Four domains · Eight situations

You cannot rate your way to self-awareness.

Ask someone to score their own empathy out of five and they will tell you what they hope is true. Ask them what they would actually say to a portfolio manager who has just been taken apart in a review, and something more honest comes out.

Eight situations. No right answers on display. At the end you get one development focus, not four, because attention that is spread across everything changes nothing.

Duration
Eight minutes, uninterrupted
Method
Behavioural choice, not self-rating
Output
A one-page brief you can print

Answer as you would actually behave on a difficult day, not as you would like to behave on a good one. The profile is only as useful as your honesty about the first.
